# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, no harassment or personal attacks, and keep
discussions about the work. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and comments that break
these rules and may ban people who keep breaking them.

Report a problem privately through https://github.com/moe-spiration/openkortex/security/advisories/new (the form is
private and reaches only the maintainers). Reports are handled confidentially.
